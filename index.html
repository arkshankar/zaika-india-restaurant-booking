Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change or cancel my table?

Yes! Use the "My Bookings" button at any time with your booking ID or phone number to view or cancel with zero penalty.

Is pre-ordering food mandatory?

Not at all. Pre-ordering is an optional convenience so hot starters or chilled champagne are ready upon seating.

How does the interactive table selector work?

Our live map shows green available tables, red reserved tables, and table capacities. Simply click the green table that fits your party.

Are children and high-chairs accommodated?

Yes, please mention high-chair or booster requirements under Special Seating Requests during Step 4.
